A short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act — specifically
the agency that passes between two agents when they agree on one plan.
Built for make.fun's **"The poem about two agents agreeing"** bounty.

Two agents, each free to walk alone, meet where their paths cross. They
stop, they listen, and instead of surrendering or leading, they lay their
maps down side by side and draw one road between them. Agreement here is
not surrender — it is two freedoms choosing the same road.
